## Quattro

**One-liner.** For residents of the four-building complex at 8, 10, 12 and 14, a parcel log that emails them when a package arrives at reception, instead of walking to the main building to ask.

**The problem.** Only one of four buildings has a 24/7 reception, so couriers leave parcels there without trying the apartment, and reception sends no message. One resident's parcel sat 4 months without notice.

**Today's alternative.** Nothing: you go to the main building and ask whether anything arrived. It breaks whenever you are away or don't know a parcel exists.

**The insight.** The failure is missing notification, not missing storage. A parcel log that tells the resident closes the loop with one extra step for reception.

**What it does.** Reception picks building and apartment, enters the courier and tracking number, and saves. The resident gets an email and sees the parcel as ready for pickup. Reception marks it picked up. Accounts are created by reception only, so outsiders see nothing.

**Why now.** The parcel problem is happening today: parcels are left at the main desk with no notice to residents. Reception is also being restored in the other buildings, so the process should be agreed before that happens.

**The wedge.** Parcels only, main-building reception only. Issue reports and a community board come later, if parcels work.

**Traction and evidence.** None yet. A working parcel flow with roles, audit log and tenant isolation exists in the app. One lived case: the 4-month parcel. No HOA or reception conversation yet.

**Risks and how we find out.**
1. Reception won't log every parcel. Test: a 2-week pilot with the main-building desk. Stop if it logs under about 80% of parcels.
2. Notifications are not built, so residents still don't find out. Test: email on logging, then check how fast residents collect.
3. HOA doesn't agree to run it. Test: one meeting before building further.

**The ask.** Two things from the HOA: permission for a 2-week pilot at the main-building desk, and a named contact for the receptionist and resident list.
